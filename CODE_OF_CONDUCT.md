# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Predicting Restaurant Net Margin from Food Delivery Orders project a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive participation includes using welcoming and inclusive language, respecting differing viewpoints and experiences, accepting constructive criticism, and showing empathy towards other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention; trolling, insults, and personal attacks; public or private harassment; sharing another person's private information without permission; and other behavior inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying acceptable behavior and taking fair, appropriate corrective action. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Consequences may include a warning, temporary restrictions on participation, or a permanent ban, depending on the severity and recurrence of the behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, and team meetings, and in public spaces when someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the project team at zheqijin@student.ubc.ca, afrahz@student.ubc.ca, jerry_zjw@outlook.com . Include a description of the incident and relevant supporting information. Do not post sensitive reports in public GitHub issues.

The team will review reports, investigate fairly, and decide on appropriate corrective action. Reports will be handled confidentially, with information shared only as needed to investigate and address the incident. If the report concerns the designated contact, contact the DSCI 522 instructional team privately through the course's established communication channels instead.

Maintainers are also bound by this policy. Complaints about a maintainer's conduct or enforcement may be escalated to the instructional team.
